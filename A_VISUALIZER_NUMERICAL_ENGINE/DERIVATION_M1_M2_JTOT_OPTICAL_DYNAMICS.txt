BKQR GR OPTICAL DYNAMICS v1.6.4.4 -- FORMAL MODEL SUMMARY

GEOMETRIZED UNITS
  G=c=1.
  M=m1+m2, mu=m1*m2/M, eta=mu/M.
  Lorb=Jtot-S1z-S2z, sigma=sign(Lorb).
  x=(M |Omega|)^(2/3).

3PN CIRCULAR BINDING ENERGY
  E(x)=-(M eta x/2) [1+e1 x+e2 x^2+e3 x^3]
  e1=-3/4-eta/12
  e2=-27/8+19 eta/8-eta^2/24
  e3=-675/64 +(34445/576-205 pi^2/96)eta
      -155 eta^2/96 -35 eta^3/5184.

3PN CIRCULAR ANGULAR MOMENTUM
Using dE=Omega dJ on the circular sequence,
  Jorb(x)=eta M^2 x^(-1/2)
    [1-2 e1 x-e2 x^2-(4/5)e3 x^3].
The code solves |Lorb|=Jorb(x0) by bisection on the weak-field branch.
Thus Jtot is no longer converted to r0 with a Newtonian J^2 rule.
  r0=M/x0,
  Omega0=sigma x0^(3/2)/M.

3.5PN NONSPINNING GW LUMINOSITY
  F(x)=(32/5) eta^2 x^5 times the standard point-mass 3.5PN circular correction
  through x^(7/2), including tail/log terms.

ENERGY / ANGULAR-MOMENTUM BALANCE
  dx/dt = -F(x)/(dE/dx),
  dphi/dt = sigma x^(3/2)/M,
  dErad/dt = F(x),
  dJrad/dt = sigma F(x)/|Omega|.
The inspiral is integrated with adaptive RK4 substeps. The finite-separation ledger is
  Madm,0 = M + E(x0),
  Msystem(t) + Erad(t) = Madm,0,
  Jsystem(t) + Jrad(t) = Jtot.

STRONG-FIELD SWITCH
The PN branch ends before dE/dx=0:
  xMECO = positive weak-field root of dE/dx=0,
  xtr = min(0.92 xMECO, 1/rSafetyFactor),
  rtr=M/xtr.
Input J is rejected if it would require a circular x0>xtr.

NR-CALIBRATED REMNANT
The final radiated fraction and dimensionless aligned final spin are the
Jimenez-Forteza et al. (2017) fits as implemented by LALSuite/PhenomX.
The finite-separation energy budget additionally enforces Mfinal<Madm,0.
The merger closure moves Msystem and Jsystem monotonically toward the fitted
remnant values without violating either ledger.

RINGDOWN SCALE
For remnant dimensionless spin chi,
  Mfinal*omega_220 = 1.5251 - 1.1568 (1-|chi|)^0.1292,
  Q_220 = 0.7000 + 1.4187 (1-|chi|)^(-0.4990),
  tau_220 = 2 Q_220 / omega_220.
This sets the characteristic post-merger timescale/frequency.

CLAIM BOUNDARY
This is a PN/NR-calibrated reduced-order evolution, not a BSSN, Z4c, or
generalized-harmonic solution of the Einstein PDEs. The merger separation envelope
is a smooth closure constrained by energy/J and NR remnant data; it is not itself a
field-equation-derived coordinate trajectory. The current optical remnant is
Schwarzschild even when the tracked remnant spin is nonzero.
